We started with a cart and a borrowed grinder

Kerbside began as a coffee cart outside number 12 on Kerb Lane: two flasks, a grinder on loan from a friend and a queue of people on their way to the station.

Today it's a café, a bakery and a small roastery, still on the same street. The cart is in the back, and on the first warm Saturday of the year it comes out again.
