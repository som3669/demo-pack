Six years on one street

2019

The cart

Two flasks, a borrowed grinder and a stall outside number 12, weekday mornings only.

2021

The shop

Number 12 came up for rent. We took the window seat for ourselves for about a week.

2023

The oven

A deck oven in the back, and bread on the shelf by half past seven.

2025

The roaster

A five-kilo roaster three doors down, and beans you can take home.
