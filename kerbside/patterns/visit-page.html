  • By bus: the 72 and 73 stop on Kerb Lane, outside the door.

  • By car: pay-and-display on Station Road, five minutes' walk.

Allergens

Everything is made in one small kitchen with gluten, nuts, milk and eggs, so we cannot promise any dish is free of them. Ask at the counter and we will tell you exactly what is in it.

Do you have gluten-free bread?

Yes, from a bakery that bakes only gluten-free. We toast it in its own toaster, but it is made in our kitchen.

Which milks do you have?

Whole, oat and almond, all at the same price. Ask for half and half if you like.

Is anything vegan?

The avocado on sourdough, the soup, the corn salad and most of the loaves. They are marked VG on the menu.

Do the cardamom buns have nuts?

No nuts in the buns, but they are baked on the same trays as the almond croissants.
