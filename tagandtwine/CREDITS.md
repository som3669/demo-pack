# Tag & Twine demo pack — credits

Tag & Twine, its products, prices, reviews, address and journal are invented. No photograph shows a
brand name, a logo, readable text or an identifiable face: where a label carried print, it was
removed, as the table says. The pack is demo content for the Yantra Pro theme.

Every photograph is **CC0 1.0**: dedicated to the public domain, free to use, change and
redistribute, including commercially, with no attribution required. Credit is given as a courtesy.
They come from rawpixel's public-domain collection (CC0), found through Openverse, and from photos
the Gadgetix, Vitrena and Luma packs already use (StockSnap and rawpixel, CC0). Each was cropped as
the table says, resized to at most 1100 pixels and recompressed. `images/sources.json` keeps each
photo's source page, creator, original and final size, and what was changed.

The logo (`images/tagandtwine-logo.png`) was drawn for the pack: a tag shape and the name set in
Gabarito (SIL Open Font License 1.1).
