Tag & Twine started in 2019 as a market stall of good basics. It’s now a small team in Portland that picks every product it sells, from tees and knitwear to headphones and mugs.

How we pick

  • We wear, use or wash everything before it goes on the shelf.
  • We pay a fair price to the people who make it, and we tell you where it comes from.
  • We keep prices low by keeping the range small: good things, and no filler.

Where your order comes from

Orders leave our warehouse in Portland, Oregon, every weekday at 3 pm, packed in recycled paper with no plastic fill.
