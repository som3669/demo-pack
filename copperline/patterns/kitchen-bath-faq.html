Questions people ask

Do you do the tiling?

We work with two tilers we've trusted for years and book them for you, so there's one schedule and one person to call: us.

How long does a bathroom take?

A swap of fixtures takes a day or two. A full bathroom is usually two to three weeks, and the quote gives you the dates.

Do I need to be home?

For the first and last day, yes. In between, a key and a code to the side door are fine.

Can you supply the fixtures?

Yes, at cost, or fit the ones you choose. We'll tell you if something won't last.
