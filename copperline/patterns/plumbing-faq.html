Questions people ask

How soon can you come?

Most days we have a same-day window if you book before 1 pm. For a burst pipe, call us on (614) 555-0142, any hour: we keep space for those.

Do you fix every brand?

Yes: faucets, toilets, disposals and water heaters, tank or tankless, gas or electric, old and new.

Can you find a leak I can't see?

Usually. We pressure-test the lines and use a moisture meter before opening any wall, so we cut one small hole, not five.

Do you clear drains too?

Yes. Drains have a page of their own, with fixed prices for sinks, tubs, showers and the main line.
