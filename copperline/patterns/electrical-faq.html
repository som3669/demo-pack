Questions people ask

Yes. We're licensed in Ohio as electrical contractors, and Marisol is a master electrician. Every job is insured.

Can you replace knob-and-tube wiring?

Yes, room by room or all at once, with as few holes as we can manage and the plaster patched afterwards.

Do you fit car chargers?

Yes, Level 2 chargers on a circuit of their own. We look at your panel first and quote in writing.

How soon can you come?
