Questions people ask

Will you use chemicals?

No. We clear drains with cables and water jets. Chemicals eat old pipes and rarely shift a real blockage.

How do I stop it happening again?

Hair catchers in the shower, no grease down the sink, and nothing but toilet paper in the toilet. That covers most of it.

Will you dig up my yard?

Only if the camera shows a broken pipe, and only after you've seen it and agreed a price.

How soon can you come?

Most days the same day if you book before 1 pm. Sewage coming up into the house: call us now, any hour.
