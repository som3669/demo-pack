# Copperline demo pack — credits

Copperline Home Services, its family, team, prices, reviews, phone number and areas are invented;
the neighborhoods and ZIP codes are Columbus, Ohio's, and the address a made-up one on a real street.
No photograph shows a brand name, a logo, readable text or an identifiable face: where one did, the
maker's name was painted out or cropped away, as the table says. The pack is demo content for the
Yantra Pro theme.

Every photograph is **CC0 1.0**: dedicated to the public domain, free to use, change and
redistribute, including commercially, with no attribution required. Credit is given as a courtesy.
They come from the WordPress Photo Directory (every photo there is CC0), rawpixel's public-domain
collection (CC0), StockSnap (CC0) and Flickr (photos marked "Public Domain Dedication (CC0)",
licence 9). Each was resized to at most 1600 pixels and recompressed. `images/sources.json` keeps
each photo's source page, creator, original and final size, and what was changed.
