How a corner bakery doubled its online orders

adminPublished September 4, 20261 min read

The bakery on the corner had a site that looked fine and sold almost nothing. Three changes over one weekend doubled its online orders in a month.

One menu, one page

The menu had been a photo of the chalkboard. Now it is a page with every bread, its price and the days it is baked, so people can search it.

An order button that never moves

The header now holds a button that says Order for pickup, on every page. Nobody has to look for it.

Photos at opening time

The owner takes one photo each morning, on her phone, when the light is best and the shelves are full. People order what they can see.
