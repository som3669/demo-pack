# Yantra demo pack — credits

The company, people and client names in this pack are invented. No photograph shows a brand name,
a logo or an identifiable face. The pack is demo content for the Yantra theme.

Every photograph comes from the [WordPress Photo Directory](https://wordpress.org/photos/) and is
**CC0 1.0**: dedicated to the public domain, free to use, change and redistribute, including
commercially, with no attribution required. Credit is given as a courtesy. Each file was cropped to
16:10, resized to 1600 × 1000 and recompressed for the pack; `images/sources.json` keeps the source
page and the exact file each one came from.

The pages are built from Yantra's own block patterns, so their words and the two illustrations on
them (an overview screen and a task list) come with the theme, under its own licence.

`images/dashboard.png` and `images/workspace.png` are Yantra's own drawings (GPL-2.0-or-later, like the theme), rendered to PNG at twice their size because WordPress does not import SVG files.
