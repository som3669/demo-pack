Ask a question
How soon will we see results?

Ads can bring leads in the first two weeks. SEO usually takes three to six months to move rankings in a real way. Your monthly report shows both, so you always know where things stand.

Do we have to sign a long contract?

No. Plans run month to month and you can cancel with 30 days' notice. Most clients stay because the numbers keep improving, not because they have to.

Is the ad budget included in the price?

No. Your ad budget goes straight to Google or Meta from your own account, so you can see every cent. The plan price covers our work.

Who will we work with?

One strategist plans and runs your account and joins your monthly call. There is no hand-off to someone new after you sign.

Do you work outside Portland?

Yes. About a third of our clients are elsewhere in Oregon and Washington. Calls happen on video and reports arrive by email.
