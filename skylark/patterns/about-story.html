Why we do it this way

Big agencies sell you a senior strategist and hand you to a junior after the contract is signed. We decided early that the person who plans your campaigns would be the person on your monthly call, every month.

That keeps us small. We take on eight new clients a month at most, and we would rather say no than do the work badly. It is also why every report fits on one page: if we cannot explain what your money did in a page, we have not understood it ourselves.
