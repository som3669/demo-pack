Billing questions

How much should we spend on ads?

Most local businesses start between $1,000 and $3,000 a month. Your free audit includes a suggested budget for your area and trade.

Is there a setup fee?

No. The first month covers setup, and campaigns go live within two weeks.

Can we change plans later?

Yes, from the next month. Move up when things are working and down in a quiet season.
