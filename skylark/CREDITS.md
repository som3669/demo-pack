# Skylark demo pack — credits

Skylark Digital, its team, clients, prices and figures are invented. No photograph shows a brand
name, a logo, readable text or an identifiable face. The pack is demo content for the Yantra Pro
theme.

Every photograph is **CC0 1.0**: dedicated to the public domain, free to use, change and
redistribute, including commercially, with no attribution required. Credit is given as a courtesy.
Each photo comes from the WordPress Photo Directory, where every photo is CC0; each was resized to at
most 1600 pixels and recompressed. `images/sources.json` keeps each photo's source page and final
size.
