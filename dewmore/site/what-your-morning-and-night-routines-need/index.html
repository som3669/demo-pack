What your morning and night routines need

adminPublished October 16, 20261 min read

Part 3 of 4 in Routine basics

A routine doesn’t need ten steps. It needs the right three or four, in the right order, at the right time of day.

Contents
  1. Mornings protect
  2. Nights repair
  3. Build yours

Mornings protect

  • A gentle cleanse, or just water.
  • A serum for what bothers you most: vitamin C suits mornings.
  • A light moisturiser.
  • Sun cream, every day.

Nights repair

  • Take off sun cream and makeup with a balm, then cleanse.
  • Retinal or an acid tonic, on separate nights.
  • A richer moisturiser.
  • A sleep mask or oil, two or three nights a week.
