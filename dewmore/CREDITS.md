# Dewmore demo pack — credits

Dewmore, its products, prices, reviews, reviewers, address and articles are invented. No
photograph shows a brand name, a logo, readable packaging text or an identifiable face. The pack
is demo content for the Yantra Pro theme.

Every photograph is **CC0 1.0**: dedicated to the public domain, free to use, change and
redistribute, including commercially, with no attribution required. Credit is given as a courtesy.
All of them are photos the Luma pack already uses (from StockSnap and Rawpixel's public-domain
collection), framed again for this pack as the table says, resized to at most 1400 pixels and
recompressed. `images/sources.json` keeps each photo's source page, creator, original and final
size, and what was changed.

The logo (`images/dewmore-logo.png`) was drawn for the pack: a droplet and the name set in
Urbanist (SIL Open Font License 1.1).
