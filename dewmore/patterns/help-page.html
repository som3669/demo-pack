Help and delivery

Delivery, returns, refills and skin types, answered. Still stuck? Ask a skin adviser, every day.

When will my order arrive?

Orders placed before 2 pm on a weekday leave the same day. Standard delivery takes two to three working days and is free over $40. In a few ZIP codes we deliver the same day.

Can I return something I’ve opened?

Yes. You have 60 days to send back anything, opened or not, for a full refund. If a product didn’t suit your skin, tell us why and we’ll suggest one that might.

How do I know my skin type?

Wash your face, wait an hour, then look in the mirror. Tight all over: dry. Shiny all over: oily. Shiny on the forehead and nose only: combination. Stings or flushes easily: sensitive. Our skin type guide has more.

How do refills work?

Keep the glass bottle, buy the refill pouch, and pour it in. Pouches are paper with a thin plant-based lining, and go in your home compost.

How does the free gift work?

Once your cart reaches $60, a travel-size oat milk cleanser goes in it at no cost. Take it out if you don’t want it.

Are your products tested on animals?

Never. We test on people with sensitive skin, who volunteer and are paid for it.
