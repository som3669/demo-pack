Dewmore started in 2021 at a kitchen table, with a cleanser that didn’t sting. Today we’re a small team in Portland who make calm, simple skincare and sheer makeup, and answer the chat ourselves.

How we make it

  • No perfume and no essential oils, in anything.
  • Short ingredient lists, each ingredient explained on the product’s page.
  • Every formula patch-tested on volunteers with sensitive skin before it goes on sale.
  • Glass bottles to keep, and refill pouches that cost less.

Where your order comes from

Orders leave our studio in Portland every weekday at 2 pm, in recycled cardboard with paper padding. In a few ZIP codes we deliver the same day.
