Skin guide

Find your skin type

Wash your face, wait an hour, then look in the mirror. How it feels and shines tells you which of these you are.

Dry

Feels tight after washing and flakes in winter. Look for creams, balms and oils, and skip strong acids.

Shop for dry skin

Oily

Shiny by midday, with larger pores. Look for gels, light fluids and niacinamide; you still need moisturiser.

Shop for oily skin

Combination

An oily forehead and nose, drier cheeks. Look for light gels, and put richer cream only where it’s dry.

Shop for combination skin

Sensitive

Stings, flushes or itches easily. Look for short ingredient lists, centella and oat, and add one new product at a time.

Shop for sensitive skin

Normal

Rarely oily, rarely tight. Most of our products suit you; pick by what you want to work on.

Shop for normal skin
