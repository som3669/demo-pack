# Daybook demo pack — credits

Daybook, its writers and its email addresses are invented. No photograph shows a brand name, a
logo, readable packaging text or an identifiable face. The pack is demo content for the Yantra Pro
theme.

Every photograph is **CC0 1.0**: dedicated to the public domain, free to use, change and
redistribute, including commercially, with no attribution required. Credit is given as a courtesy.
The photos were already vetted for the eBasket, Luma and Yantra packs in this repository; for
Daybook each was cropped (some to a square or a portrait shape, for the Masonry layout), resized
to at most 1600 pixels and recompressed. `images/sources.json` keeps each photo's source page, the
pack it came from and its final size.
