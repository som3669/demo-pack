# Fullcharge demo pack — credits

Fullcharge, its brands (Altis, Corvo, Mira, Pell, Kestrel, Hollis, Pixelpine), products, prices,
reviews, address and buying guides are invented. No photograph shows a brand name, a logo,
readable text or an identifiable face: where a product carried a logo or print, it was painted
out, as the table says. The pack is demo content for the Yantra Pro theme.

Every photograph is **CC0 1.0**: dedicated to the public domain, free to use, change and
redistribute, including commercially, with no attribution required. Credit is given as a courtesy.
They come from rawpixel's public-domain collection (CC0), found through Openverse, from the
WordPress Photo Directory (CC0), and from photos the Gadgetix and Vitrena packs already use
(StockSnap, rawpixel and the WordPress Photo Directory, CC0). Each was cropped as the table says,
resized to at most 1200 pixels and recompressed. `images/sources.json` keeps each photo's source
page, creator, original and final size, and what was changed.

The logo (`images/fullcharge-logo.png`) and the brand logos (`images/brand-*.png`) were drawn for
the pack: a battery shape and the names set in Readex Pro and Hanken Grotesk (SIL Open Font
License 1.1).
