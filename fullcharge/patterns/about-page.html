Fullcharge started in 2020 as two people fixing phones above a bike shop. It’s now a small team in Austin that tests every laptop, phone and pair of headphones it sells.

How we pick

  • We use everything for a week before it goes on the shelf, and we say what we didn’t like.
  • We stock a few good models at each price, not every model there is.
  • We tell you when the cheaper one will do.

Where your order comes from

Orders leave our warehouse in Austin, Texas, every weekday at 3 pm, in recycled cardboard with paper padding.
