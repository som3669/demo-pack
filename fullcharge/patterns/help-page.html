Help and returns

Delivery, returns, warranty and cases, answered. Still stuck? We’re here every day.

When will my order arrive?

Orders placed before 3 pm on a weekday leave the same day. Standard delivery takes two to three working days and is free over $80; Express arrives the next working day.

How do I return something?

Start a return from your account within 30 days, print the label and drop the parcel at any post office. Returns are free, and we refund within three days of getting it back.

What does the 2-year warranty cover?

Faults in laptops and phones for two years from delivery, and in everything else for one. We collect it, fix or replace it, and bring it back.

Which case fits my phone?

Every case lists the phones it fits, and the case finder on the home page shows only the ones made for yours.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes, until it leaves the warehouse. Write to us with your order number and we’ll change it for you.

Do you ship outside the US?

Not yet. We deliver to every US state, including Alaska and Hawaii (three to six days there).

Still stuck?

Email or call us every day from 8 am to 10 pm: we answer within a working day.
