# Yantra demo pack (Tilework) — credits

Tilework, its team, its clients (Halden, Mossgrove, Quillo, Brisa, Tidal, Parcel & Pine) and every
quote in this pack are invented. No photograph shows a brand name, a logo or an identifiable face.
The pack is demo content for the Yantra theme.

The pack's words, block patterns (`patterns/`) and stylesheet (`tilework.css`) were written for it
and are **CC0 1.0**, like the pictures below.

## Logo and case-study pictures — `images/tilework-logo.png`, `images/work/`

The Tilework mark (four rounded tiles) and the six case-study pictures (app and website screens,
the Brisa identity) were drawn for this pack as HTML mockups and rendered to images. They show only
invented brands and are **CC0 1.0**.

## Photographs

Every photograph comes from the [WordPress Photo Directory](https://wordpress.org/photos/) and is
**CC0 1.0**: dedicated to the public domain, free to use, change and redistribute, including
commercially, with no attribution required. Credit is given as a courtesy. Each file was resized and
recompressed for the pack (the post photos were also cropped to 16:10); `images/sources.json` keeps
the source page and the exact file each one came from.
