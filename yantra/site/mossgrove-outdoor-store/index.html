Mossgrove: an outdoor store built for phones

adminPublished October 28, 20261 min read

Mossgrove makes jackets, packs and boots for people who walk a long way. Their old store looked good on a laptop and fell apart on a phone.

  • Client: Mossgrove, outdoor clothing and kit
  • Services: e-commerce design, development, growth
  • Timeline: 10 weeks
  • Platform: WordPress and WooCommerce

The brief

Visitors on phones were adding to the bag and leaving at checkout. Mossgrove wanted a store that sold as well on a phone on a train as on a desk at home, without moving off WordPress.

What we did

  • Watched twelve customers shop on their own phones
  • Redesigned product pages around photos, sizes and stock left
  • Cut checkout from five steps to two, with wallets first
  • Made every page load in under two seconds on a slow connection

A year later

  • 38% more of mobile visits end in an order
  • Average order up from €118 to €142
  • Returns for the wrong size down by a third, thanks to a fit guide

They asked better questions about our customers than we did. The new store paid for itself in the first season.

Tom Marsh, Founder, Mossgrove
